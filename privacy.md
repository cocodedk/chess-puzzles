# Privacy Policy — Chess Puzzles

**App:** Chess Puzzles (`dk.cocode.chess`)
**Developer:** CoCode.dk — Babak Bandpey
**Last updated:** 7 October 2026

> The canonical, always-current version of this policy (English and Persian) is published at
> **https://chess.cocode.dk/privacy/**, and in Danish at **https://chess.cocode.dk/da/privacy/**

**Chess Puzzles does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.**
It is a chess tactics trainer that runs entirely on your device. It needs no account and no
sign-up, and the app itself has no internet access.

## Your progress stays on your device

So you can pick up where you left off, the app saves a small amount of data on your device: the
number of puzzles you have solved, how many of those you solved without a hint, your current and best
streak of puzzles in a row, your day streak (how many days in a row you have solved a puzzle) and the
date you last solved one, which puzzle you are on, how many times you have played each opening from
memory without a mistake, and your theme choice (Match phone, Light or Dark). This is kept in a private
data store on your own phone. The app never sends it to us or to any third party. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes
the copy on your phone; a device backup (see below) may keep a separate copy.

## No internet, no tracking

- The app **never asks you for a permission** and has **no internet permission**, so it cannot send your
  data anywhere. It does declare one permission of its own, added by an Android library, that Android grants
  only to apps signed with the same key as this one. It gives no access to your data, your phone or the internet, and you are never asked to
  approve it.
- We use **no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising**.
- There are **no third-party tracking SDKs, no cookies, and no advertising identifiers**.
- There is **no account and no sign-up**; you never provide us with any information.

## The puzzles

Every puzzle is bundled inside the app, sampled from the
[Lichess Open Database](https://database.lichess.org) (CC0 1.0). Solving puzzles happens entirely
offline and creates no record anywhere except the progress saved on your own device.

## Device backup

If you have enabled Android Auto Backup or Google account backup on your device, the operating system
may include this app's local data (your progress and theme choice) in your own personal Google
backup, and may restore it if you reinstall the app or set up a new phone. That copy is separate from
the one on your phone, so deleting the app does not delete it. Whether a backup happens depends on the app's backup setting and your device and account
settings. We have no access to the backup. See
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details.

## External links

The app's About screen has buttons that lead to web pages: the app's website and this privacy policy
(chess.cocode.dk), the app's page on F-Droid, the source code and the issue tracker on
[GitHub](https://github.com/cocodedk/chess-puzzles), the developer's website ([cocode.dk](https://cocode.dk))
and the developer's LinkedIn profile. A page opens only when you tap its button. The app hands the
address to Android, which opens it in your browser or in another app that handles that kind of link. The
app itself never connects to those sites, and it sends nothing to them and receives nothing from them.
Once a page is open, it belongs to that site, and that site's own privacy policy applies.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
