About Chess Puzzles

Chess Puzzles helps you practise chess tactics. Each puzzle comes from the Lichess puzzle database, tells you what to find (for example, checkmate in 2) and has a rating that shows how hard it is. You can also learn common openings move by move, then play them from memory. Everything is stored on your phone, so the app works without internet.
