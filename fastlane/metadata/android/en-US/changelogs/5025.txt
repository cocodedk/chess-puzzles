TalkBack now outlines each square exactly as it is drawn, also on a small board. After text that is no move, "Not a legal move here" no longer stays on the line when you ask for a hint. With the phone held sideways, Hint and Restart share a row and so do Previous and Next, so Type a move is in view without scrolling. The Puzzles and Openings tabs no longer jump when you switch between them. The app is now built for Android 16.
