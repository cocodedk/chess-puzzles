<p>Chess Puzzles is an offline tactics trainer built from real positions in the Lichess puzzle database. Find the best move, follow the tactical line, and improve at your own pace.</p>

<p><b>Accessibility.</b> Made to work with weak sight, or none.</p>

<ul>
<li>Works with TalkBack, Android's screen reader. It reads where every piece stands, reads the square you touch, and says each move in words, such as "knight takes e5, check".</li>
<li>Type a move such as Nf3, or say it with your keyboard's voice typing, instead of finding a small square. (On the Puzzles screen.)</li>
<li>Buttons, menus and messages follow the text size you set on your phone.</li>
<li>On an upright phone the board is as wide as the screen, and its letters and numbers stay readable.</li>
<li>Light or dark theme.</li>
</ul>

<ul>
<li><b>Clear goals.</b> Each puzzle tells you what to find, such as checkmate in two.</li>
<li><b>Three difficulty bands.</b> Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard puzzles.</li>
<li><b>Useful hints.</b> Reveal the move to play when you need a nudge.</li>
<li><b>Opening practice.</b> Learn twenty openings move by move, then practise them from memory against selected replies.</li>
<li><b>Saved progress.</b> Track solved puzzles, puzzles solved without a hint, and streaks on your device.</li>
<li><b>Light and dark themes.</b> Use the board that suits your screen.</li>
<li><b>Fully offline.</b> Every puzzle is bundled with the app.</li>
</ul>

<p>The app needs no account. It never asks you for a permission and cannot use the internet. It contains no ads, analytics, or tracking. Puzzles come from the Lichess Open Database under CC0 1.0, and opening names from Lichess's opening list, also CC0 1.0.</p>
